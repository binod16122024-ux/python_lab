Scenario
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A byte list was XOR-encoded with a key. Decode it to recover the flag.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Task 1 - Decode
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Points   : 20

Objective:
  Decode 'encoded' by XOR-ing each byte with 'key' and converting the
  results back into characters.

Question:
  Submit the flag the decoded bytes spell out.

Hint:
  XOR is its own inverse: (x ^ key) ^ key == x. Use chr()/ord().
