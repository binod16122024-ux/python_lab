Scenario
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The flag is hidden inside a string, woven together character-by-character
with decoy letters. You can't spot it by eye - you have to pull it apart
in code.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Task 1 - Extract
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Points   : 20

Objective:
  'mixed' interleaves the real flag with decoy characters: one real
  character, then one decoy character, repeating for the whole string.
  Extract every other character, starting from the first, to recover the
  flag.

Question:
  Submit the flag hidden inside 'mixed'.

Hint:
  A slice with a step of 2 pulls out every other character:
  mixed[::2] starts at index 0 and skips one character each time.
